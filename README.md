## ⚡ Key Business Takeaways

* **📈 Peak Growth & Regulations:** Listings peaked in **2015**. Airbnb reached profitability in **H2 2016** prior to the 2020 COVID-19 decline.
* **🌆 Market Dominance:** **Paris, NYC, and Sydney** generate **50% of all listings** and **59% of reviews**. Paris hotel prices ($800) average nearly **2x higher** than an Airbnb Private Room ($462).
* **⭐ Satisfaction Drivers:** **Mexico City & Rio** lead in user satisfaction. **Cleanliness & Value** represent the primary bottleneck across lower-rated markets (Hong Kong, Istanbul).
* **🛡️ Platform Trust:** **66.9%** of hosts are fully verified with a profile photo; unverified profiles remain under **0.4%**.

## 🛠️ Power BI Design & Technical Skills

- **Dynamic Navigation:** Custom bookmark buttons 
    (e.g., *Star / Glass lens* toggles) to switch between summary and granular rating heatmaps seamlessly.
    
- **Advanced Visuals:** Pareto analysis (City Market Share vs. Cumulative Share)
    and Ribbon charts for monthly seasonality shifts.
    
- **DAX & Data Modeling:** Calculated measures for cumulative review frequencies,
    normalized occupancy ratings, and pricing differentials.
    
- **User Experience (UX):** Clean narrative callout boxes alongside key visual charts for immediate stakeholder decision-making.
